The New Normal

April 5, 2026

People seem to forget how boring things were before 2020. 😴

Corporate Memphis1 was embraced as progress.

Now we get to see actual progress with the democratization of taste.

When we look back at recent periods of excitement in tech, we see a mixed bag of success and failure2, but one thing is clear: "taste" seems to be a key factor in determining success.

Let's define taste as the ability to discern what's considered "good" from "bad," for any given category.

You can have taste for anything and everything, but traditionally that meant crafting your own taste by trial and error.

LLMs have changed that, by training on what developers consider to be "good" code, due to it having made it to production or a working state.

So effectively, we created a new floor for taste, and that floor is the sum total of all the "good" code that has ever been written.

This is interesting, because previously to be able to code anything meant having to learn the basics of coding, and then putting that into practice, which developed your taste.

No one could release anything without this base-level of taste, because without it, you likely wouldn't be able to ship anything due to the lack of technical knowledge required to do so.

Now that the floor has risen to a new level, people who previously didn't have the "taste" (experience) to code can actually produce something with code, due to our new level of abstraction.

That shift is giving developers a full-on identity crisis, because most assumed that being able to code was the main requirement for being a developer.

But AI removes the veil and reveals that memorizing syntax is not the same as producing value.

This is actually wild, because you no longer have to be God-tier John Carmack with 69 years of experience to produce something of value.

You can be a 10 year old kid and write two sentences3!

There has never been a more fun time to produce something with code. That's a new normal that is going to lead to an explosion of creativity and innovation.

You can almost feel the AGI. 🤯
